System Limitations

Bottlenecks identified through the two-week autoethnographic deployment.

01
Form Factor & The Hawthorne Effect
Head-mounting induced constant awareness; chest-mounting mitigated this, but hardware presence remains a barrier.
02
Third-Party Social Boundaries
Personal privacy is unproblematic, but filming others (e.g., video calls) creates severe social friction requiring manual intervention.
03
The Traceability Problem
Rolling memory compression inevitably destroys raw details. Tracing a high-level insight back to its origin is currently impossible.
04
VLM Over-Interpretation
Current vision models struggle with passive objects, forcibly imposing profound narratives onto meaningless physical clutter.
