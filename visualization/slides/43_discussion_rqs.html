Discussion: Answering the RQs

Extracting deep academic insights from the autoethnographic experience.

RQ1
Visibility &
Blind Spots
FINDING
Captures crucial intermediate states (hesitation, wandering) and unconscious habits (forgotten coffee post-focus) entirely absent from digital traces.
REFLECTION
"Rolling memory" only maps surface statistics. VLMs heavily over-interpret passive objects. True insights require dedicated reflection & deduction modules, not just data accumulation.
RQ2
Improvement
vs. Noise
PEAK
Personalization peaks when the agent bridges recording gaps (inferring offline activities) and offers timely, context-aware proactive care (meal reminders).
NOISE
Major noise stems from VLM hallucinations (fabricating actions). Furthermore, a proactive agent requires interaction memory; otherwise, care degrades into repetitive spam.
RQ3
Emerging
Design Rules
REQ 1 & 2
Capture Everything + Filter Automatically. Manual review is impossible. The system heavily relies on mid-tier "Insights", which necessitates a traceable path back to raw logs for human correction.
REQ 3
The Physical Kill Switch. While the wearer grows accustomed to the lens, preserving the privacy of third parties (e.g., during video calls) absolutely mandates a physical disruption mechanism.
